Terms and privacy

Plain-language rules for buying from Outcast. Questions? DM us on Instagram @outcastt.in.

Ordering and payment

  • Prices are in Indian rupees. The shipping cost is shown before you pay.
  • You can pay the full amount online by UPI (GPay, PhonePe or Paytm). After you order, we send the payment details to the mobile number you gave, and ship once it's paid.
  • Or choose cash on delivery and pay when the parcel arrives. Cash on delivery costs ₹100 extra.
  • We never ask for your UPI PIN or card details. Check the name on the payment request before you pay.

Shipping

  • We ship anywhere in India. Prepaid orders are dispatched after payment is received; cash on delivery orders after we confirm them with you.
  • You get a tracking link once your parcel leaves us.
  • If your pincode isn't served by our courier, we'll tell you before you pay or before we dispatch.

Returns, exchanges and cancellations

  • Custom pieces are made to order for you, so they can't be returned or cancelled once printing starts.
  • If a piece arrives damaged, or isn't what you ordered, DM us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order ID, and we'll put it right.
  • You can cancel a prepaid order before you pay, or a cash on delivery order before it's dispatched.

Privacy

What we collect: your name, mobile number, delivery address, and your Instagram handle if you give it. For custom requests, your idea.

Why: only to process, deliver and support your order. We don't sell your details or use them for ads.

Who handles it:

  • Web3Forms passes your order from this site to our email inbox.
  • Your UPI app (GPay, PhonePe or Paytm) processes online payments. Outcast never sees your UPI PIN.
  • Our courier gets your name, phone and address to deliver the parcel.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may keep standard server logs.

Your bag: pieces you add to your bag are saved only in your own browser on this device. We don't see them until you send an order.

How long: we keep order details only as long as needed for the order, returns and our records.

Your choice: DM us on Instagram to see or delete the details we hold about you.
